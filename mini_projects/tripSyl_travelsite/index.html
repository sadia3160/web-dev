Top three activities to do at Sylhet

Escape into the nature

From tea gardens to forests, hills, mountains, rivers, and haors—Sylhet is blessed with all. With comfortable weather and a peaceful atmosphere, you can immerse yourself into the nature.

Taste delicious local food

Sylhet has its own cuisine. Shatkora-mangsho, akhni, different types of pithas, and so on. Go to traditional, local restaurants and fill your tummy with delicious foods.

Visit museam and cultural sites

Sylhet is unique from every angle. They got their own language, Nagri, and their own culture and traditions. Visit cultural sites and museums to know more about the people and culture of Sylhet.
